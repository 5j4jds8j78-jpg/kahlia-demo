How it works

What the installment contract fixes
The price in hryvnias on the date of the first payment, a dated schedule and a 0.1% daily late fee. Early repayment has no fee.
How to get eOselia for an apartment in Kakhlia
Apply in Diia, get the bank's preliminary decision, reserve the apartment with us and sign at the partner bank. Usually 3–5 weeks.
Can I combine the installment plan and eOselia
Yes: the down payment goes through the developer's plan, the rest is a loan after completion. We calculate it individually.
